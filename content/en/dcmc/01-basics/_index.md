# Chapter 1 · Basics

> Every complex system, at the end of the day, is simply the repetition of simple rules.

This chapter covers the **prerequisites** for the Minecraft internal chapters that follow: binary, boolean logic, basic gate circuits, adders, the CPU/memory model, and more.

These concepts appear repeatedly in later work on save parsing, chunk generation, network protocol analysis, and the block update system.
