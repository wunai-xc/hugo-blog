# Chapter 2 · Advanced

> Once you have the basic blocks, you can start building complex structures.

This chapter covers the **general-purpose algorithms and data structures** that appear most often inside Minecraft's core systems: sorting, searching, RNG & noise functions, graphs / pathfinding, spatial indices, and more. They are the prerequisite to understanding Chunk Generation, Entity AI Navigation, Render Chunk Culling and other sub-systems at the source-code level.
